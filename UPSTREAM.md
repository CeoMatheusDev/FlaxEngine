## Synchronization policy

When importing upstream changes:

1. Record the upstream commit or release tag used as the base.
2. Prefer small, reviewable synchronization merges.
3. Keep fork-specific changes identifiable and avoid silently rewriting upstream history.
4. Preserve upstream copyright, trademark, attribution and third-party license notices.
5. Review changed third-party dependencies and their licenses before merging or distributing.
6. Test the resulting fork on the platforms and configurations affected by the merge.

Do not describe this fork as current with upstream unless the relevant upstream commit has actually been merged.
