## Maintenance rules

1. Preserve all pre-existing copyright, SPDX, and license headers.
2. Add the notice only when authorship and rights are clear.
3. For a modified upstream file, do not claim ownership of the whole file. If appropriate and legally permitted, identify only the original modification and its author separately.
4. Do not add this header to generated files, vendored code, or files whose license requires a specific header format.
5. Update the year range only for years in which the covered original material was actually created or modified.
